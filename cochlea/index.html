Model notes. This is a teaching model with realistic proportions, not a hydrodynamic simulation. Place map: Greenwood (1990), f = 165.4(100.06x − 0.88). OHC gain up to about 60 dB at mid–high CFs and about 35 dB near the apex, with about 0.2 dB/dB BM compression from 30 to 100 dB SPL (Ruggero et al., 1997). Loss of the active tip moves the passive peak about half an octave basally. IHC AC/DC ratio falls above about 1 kHz (Palmer & Russell, 1986). Phase locking weakens above about 2 kHz and is negligible by about 5 kHz. DPOAE settings are f2/f1 = 1.22 and L1 = L2 + 10 dB (Kummer et al., 1998). The dead-region concept and TEN(HL) test follow Moore (2004). Real OAE levels and travel times vary between individuals.
