A different route to hearing

Trace sound from an external processor to an implanted electrode array and the auditory nerve.

Two parts, one system

External

Microphone · processor · coil

Worn outside the body; captures and transmits sound information.

Implanted

Receiver–stimulator · electrode array

Placed surgically; delivers electrical stimulation inside the cochlea.

Conceptual component map. Device designs and electrode configurations vary.
